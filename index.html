Match Day Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Please carry a printed copy or an active digital QR code of your e-ticket. Barcodes will be scanned at turnstiles for entry validation.

ID Proof Requirement

An official government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving Licence, or Passport) matching the primary ticket holder's name is mandatory.

Prohibited Items

Coins, power banks, lighters, flammable materials, outside food/beverages, and professional recording equipment are strictly disallowed inside the stadium.
