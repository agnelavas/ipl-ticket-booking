Stadium Entry & Ticket Guidelines

1. Gate Entry & Timing

Stadium gates open 3 hours prior to match start. Carry a valid government photo ID matching your booking name.

2. Digital M-Ticket Scan

Present your QR-coded digital pass at the turnstile. Screenshots are valid only with clear barcode brightness.

3. Prohibited Items

Power banks, outside food, helmets, umbrellas, and large backpacks are not permitted inside stadium stands.
